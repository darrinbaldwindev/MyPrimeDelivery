# Marketing Role & Next Actions

**Owner:** Marketing Overseer
**Reconciled:** 2026-09-14

## Verified proposition

MyPrimeDelivery is an Amazon-focused product and category discovery site. The owner-defined purpose is to surface top Amazon products/categories that are eligible for Prime delivery. WordPress is the owner-selected site platform.

This is not a courier, dispatch or last-mile delivery service.

## Marketing job

Marketing should help ordinary shoppers answer four questions quickly:

1. What are the strongest products in this category?
2. Which products have evidence-backed Prime eligibility?
3. Which deals are genuinely current rather than stale urgency?
4. Why was a product selected, and how fresh is the supporting evidence?

## Core positioning direction

The useful differentiation is not simply 'Amazon products'. It is a cleaner discovery layer combining:

- curated category browsing;
- explicit ranking/selection rationale once the methodology is approved;
- evidence-aware Prime status;
- time-sensitive deals with freshness/expiry controls;
- buying guides and comparison content;
- governed outbound Amazon CTAs rather than local checkout.

## Current content/funnel priorities

Safe non-production priorities:

- homepage messaging around Prime-eligible product discovery without claiming unverified eligibility;
- category landing-page templates for the 12 approved launch categories;
- 'Top Products' and 'Deals Now' component concepts using synthetic/research-only data;
- buying-guide topic architecture;
- SEO/AEO information architecture that avoids thin category pages;
- trust language explaining checked-at/evidence/stale states;
- affiliate-disclosure placement planning without publishing live affiliate links.

## Claims boundary

Do not claim:

- a product is currently Prime eligible unless authoritative current evidence supports it;
- a product is a current bestseller/top-ranked item unless the selected ranking method and current evidence support it;
- a sale, price drop, percentage discount or end time from expired/stale research;
- customer traction, conversion, revenue, savings achieved or market leadership without evidence;
- current Amazon price/stock/rating from unauthorised or stale sources.

Public deal trackers, editorial sources and historical Amazon event pages are discovery evidence only. They may inspire candidate research and content planning but cannot promote a record to live QUALIFIED status.

## Immediate next actions

1. develop reusable homepage/category/product/deal copy against synthetic fixtures;
2. prepare SEO/AEO topic clusters for the 12 launch categories;
3. maintain a claim/evidence register for all Prime/rank/deal language;
4. coordinate with the product/evidence pipeline so marketing components fail closed when evidence is UNKNOWN, STALE, EXPIRED or BLOCKED;
5. wait for authorised marketplace, ranking methodology, Amazon data-provider and affiliate-publication decisions before live commercial claims.

## Boundary

Marketing may optimise presentation and acquisition strategy, but it does not become the source of truth for product eligibility, ranking, price, stock, deal status or freshness. Evidence controls those claims.
